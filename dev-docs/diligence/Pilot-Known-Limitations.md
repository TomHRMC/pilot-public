# Known Limitations — the consolidated ledger

*The known-incomplete and deliberately-deferred items across the whole system, in one place. I publish this on purpose: a build whose author can't enumerate its own weaknesses isn't one the author understands. It's the punch list I'd hand a team on day one; I expect a fresh reviewer to add to it. Categories: **Additive** (reversible, a bounded task), **Material** (needs real work before production), **Inherent** (expected of a solo R&D build that was never operated).*

## How to read this list
- **Additive** items (2–11, 17–26, 28–30) are reversible migrations or cleanups — each a bounded task, none a foundation to rebuild.
- The **Material** items — offline-sync (#1) and the voice model's fine-tune / validation step (#16) — are senior-grade in design with known, specific completion punch lists; the route-level tenant checks (#27) are a small fix that must land before any release; offline-sync is the riskiest unfinished surface, which is why it's item #1.
- **Inherent** items (12–15) are the expected shape of a solo build that was hibernated pre-launch by design, not defects.

## What this demonstrates
The transferable skill on display isn't that the build is flawless — it isn't, and no fast build is. It's that the person who built it keeps the known weakness surface in view and names each item precisely, with a plan for each. That's the judgment that keeps an AI-augmented team producing correct work instead of confident debt.
